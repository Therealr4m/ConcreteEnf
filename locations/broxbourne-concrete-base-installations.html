Broxbourne Professional Concrete Base Installations

Our team installs concrete bases in Broxbourne for homeowners preparing their gardens for a new outdoor building. We check the building specification, ground and access before agreeing the work.

Detached family homes can have plenty of rear garden space but a narrow route alongside the house. We measure that route first so excavation, spoil removal and concrete delivery are planned around the space available.

Our concrete base installation service covers the preparation beneath the slab as well as the finished surface. You get a base planned for the building going on top.

Concrete Base Work Planned Around Your Broxbourne Property

We set out the base using the building supplier’s required footprint and tolerances. Finished height is agreed against the surrounding garden so the base supports the building without creating an awkward step or directing surface water towards it.

Where excavation exposes soft ground, we assess what needs removing and how the sub-base should be built up. Pouring over weak spots can leave the base settling unevenly and put strain on the structure above.

Narrow side paths affect which equipment can reach the work area. We check gates, turns and the distance to the base before choosing how to move materials, including whether concrete pumping is practical.

At occupied family homes, we agree delivery timing and keep the work route contained. Children and pets need to stay clear of excavation and fresh concrete, so access arrangements are settled before work starts.

How We Carry Out Your Concrete Base Installation

We confirm the position and dimensions, then excavate to the depth required for the agreed base construction. Unsuitable material is removed before the sub-base is placed and compacted in layers.

Formwork sets the edges and finished level. Slab thickness, reinforcement and any membrane requirements are agreed to suit the building and site; we check the preparation before pouring.

We place and finish the concrete, then explain how to protect it while it cures. Building installation is scheduled around the concrete’s required strength, rather than assuming it is ready as soon as the surface feels hard.

Your programme includes the excavation, concrete delivery and curing period. Base size, ground conditions and the route through the property determine how long the work takes.
