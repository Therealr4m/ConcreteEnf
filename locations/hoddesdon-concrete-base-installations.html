Back Garden Concrete Base Installations Hoddesdon

Our team carries out concrete base installation in Hoddesdon for homeowners preparing their gardens for a new outdoor building. We handle the groundwork, preparation and concrete installation, with the finished base planned around the size and position of the intended structure.

Many suburban properties around Hoddesdon have their main garden access running down the side of the house. On some jobs, the pathway is narrow enough that moving excavated soil, aggregates and equipment through it needs to be planned before work starts.

We assess the working area, existing ground levels and access route before deciding how the installation should be organised. This is particularly useful where an old paved area has been removed or the ground has been disturbed by previous garden work.

  • Checking the proposed base position and available garden access
  • Excavating the area and removing unsuitable ground material
  • Preparing and compacting the sub-base
  • Setting out the formwork and checking finished levels
  • Installing reinforcement where appropriate and pouring the concrete
  • Finishing the surface and allowing the concrete to cure before the building is installed

Ground preparation and long-term stability

A Properly Prepared Concrete Base Helps Prevent Movement

A concrete base needs suitable ground underneath it, not just a reasonably flat surface on top. If loose soil, old paving material or poorly compacted ground is left beneath the slab, the base can move or crack as the ground settles.

This is worth considering in Hoddesdon gardens where previous landscaping has changed the original ground levels or where an old structure has already been removed. The visible surface does not always show what is underneath, so the preparation needs to reflect the condition of the ground once it has been exposed.

We prepare the area before the concrete is poured, making sure the supporting layer is suitable for the proposed installation. The depth of excavation and sub-base preparation will depend on the ground, the dimensions of the base and the intended load.

Drainage also needs to be considered around the finished slab. Water should not be left collecting against the base or directed towards the property, and the finished levels need to suit the surrounding garden.

Cutting corners below the concrete can create expensive problems later because movement or cracking may mean more than a simple surface repair. Getting the groundwork right from the beginning is the more practical approach.

Our installation process

How We Install Concrete Bases in Hoddesdon

Each job is planned around the garden layout and the size of the proposed base. Once access and the working area have been assessed, we can establish the preparation required and organise the work in the correct order.

Typical installation stages

  • Reviewing the measurements, proposed position and access route
  • Clearing the working area and excavating to the required depth
  • Removing unsuitable material and preparing the ground
  • Installing and compacting the sub-base
  • Setting the formwork and checking levels
  • Adding reinforcement where required and arranging the concrete pour
  • Finishing the concrete and allowing suitable curing time

The time needed depends on the dimensions, ground conditions and ease of access. A clear garden with straightforward access is different from a job where everything has to be moved through a narrow side passage or additional excavation is needed.

Before booking, it helps to have the approximate length and width of the base, its intended position and a description of the access available. Photos of the garden and side passage can also help us understand the job before discussing the next steps.
