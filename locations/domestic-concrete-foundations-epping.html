Local property conditions

Domestic Foundation Work We Carry Out Around Epping

A lot of the residential work we see around Epping is on detached and semi-detached properties, where there is often reasonable garden space but the new foundation still has to be installed carefully alongside an existing home.

Open access is useful on these sites because it can allow materials to be brought directly into the working area instead of having everything moved through the house. We look at that access before starting so the excavation and concrete delivery can be organised without creating unnecessary disruption around the property.

Ground conditions also need to be considered. Clay-heavy soil can move as moisture levels change, so simply digging out and pouring concrete without preparing the ground properly can create problems later. The foundation needs to be built on ground that has been prepared for the intended use.

Our installation process

How We Install Domestic Concrete Foundations in Epping

The first part of a foundation job is getting the site prepared properly. We assess the area, establish the required footprint and look at how excavation and concrete delivery will work around the property.

The foundation area is then excavated to the required depth and the ground is prepared before the concrete is placed. Where the ground needs additional preparation, this is dealt with before the pour begins.

Once the preparation is ready, the foundation is formed and the concrete is poured into the prepared area. We work methodically through the pour and finishing stages so the foundation is left ready for the next stage of the domestic build.

Keeping the site organised

Planning Foundation Work Around Your Epping Property

Domestic foundation work has to fit around the existing property as well as the construction itself. We plan where excavation will take place, how materials will reach the working area and how the site can remain as tidy and accessible as possible while the work is underway.

Properties with open access give us more options for getting materials directly to the work area. On detached and semi-detached properties, this can make the installation more straightforward and helps avoid unnecessary movement of materials through the home.

We also consider the surrounding garden and existing surfaces before work begins. The aim is to keep the working area controlled.

If you already have plans, measurements or drawings for the proposed structure, send them over when you enquire. They can help us understand the foundation requirement and discuss the practical installation before arranging the work.

Foundation considerations

Why Proper Ground Preparation Matters

A concrete foundation can look fine when it has first been installed, but the important part is what is happening underneath it. If the supporting ground has not been prepared correctly, movement can develop after the concrete has been poured.

Clay-heavy ground is one of the reasons preparation needs to be taken seriously around Epping. Changes in moisture can cause the ground to expand or contract, and that movement can become an issue if the foundation has not been installed on properly prepared ground.

We do not try to save time by cutting corners during excavation or preparation. Problems below a completed foundation are much harder to deal with once the structure above has been built.
